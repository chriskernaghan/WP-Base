In short

We are the person an investor here can actually meet

Investment decisions are made in conversations, over quarters, by people who need a local answer to an awkward question. Representation puts someone credible in those rooms on your behalf, and keeps the thread alive between visits.

Who it's for

You don't need an office here. You need someone here

Economic development organisations, regional governments and investment promotion agencies that want a credible presence in this market without setting up an office and hiring locally.

The work

What we actually do

i / Targeting

Find the companies worth talking to

Not a scraped list. We work from sector knowledge and our own network to identify businesses with a real reason to look at your region — a customer, a supply chain, a talent problem you can solve.

ii / Approach

Get in the room

We make the approach ourselves, in your name. Most of the value of a local representative is that the first email comes from someone the recipient already knows.

iii / Momentum

Hold the conversation together

Between visits, nothing dies quietly in somebody’s inbox. We chase, brief and prepare so momentum survives the gaps.

iv / Reporting

Report in a usable form

Short written updates, one proper quarterly review, and a pipeline you can take to a board without translating it first.

What you get

A qualified pipeline, named meetings, and an address in market

A target list reviewed each quarter. Introductions and meetings actually held, not just requested. Visit and mission programmes run end to end. Intelligence on the regions you're competing against. A local phone number and address so enquiries land somewhere real.

The engagement

How it works

Contracts usually run two to three years, because relationships of this kind take time to compound. We scale the programme to your ambition.

01

A scoping call

Forty minutes. What you’re mandated to deliver, what’s been tried, and whether this is the right service at all.

02

A target-sector review

We come back with the sectors where your region has a genuine case — and the ones where it doesn’t.

03

A programme sized to the mandate

From a focused lead-generation effort through to a full team with events and marketing support behind it.

Often run alongside

Representation rarely travels alone

Trade development representation works the same network in the opposite direction. Missions give the pipeline a reason to meet in person. Proposition development makes sure what you're saying is worth hearing before we start saying it.
