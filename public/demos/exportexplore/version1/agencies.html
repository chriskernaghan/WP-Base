Your team in the UK and Ireland

Most agencies know exactly what they want from this market. What's harder is having someone credible on the ground who can find the right companies, open the doors and keep going when the first answer is no. That's the job we do.

What you're probably dealing with

You have a mandate to be present in a market you can't afford an office in

Perhaps a representation contract is coming up for renewal and you'd like to test the market. Perhaps a new strategy has put the UK and Ireland on the list for the first time. Perhaps you have companies asking for help here and no practical way to support them. Or perhaps you simply need results you can show a board, a governor or an elected member. Whichever it is, you need someone who understands that your world runs on evidence and scrutiny, not enthusiasm.

How it runs

One retainer, four moving parts

Scoping first, so we agree what success looks like. Then a qualified pipeline built from our own network, in-market activity to move it, and a reporting rhythm you can put in front of a minister without rewriting it.

What it's like to work with us

The people you meet are the people who do the work

A senior consultant who knows your region, agreed activity levels, and quarterly reporting you can take straight to your board without translating it first. When your team comes to market, we host you. When something isn't working, we tell you.
